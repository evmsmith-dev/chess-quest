      What's stored?

      Only what's needed to play: the moves, each player's avatar, the clock times, the latest emoji reaction (one of six, chosen from a list: there's no typing), and a random player number made by the app (not linked to any account, email or name). A saved friend is a private link only your two devices know; the nickname you give a friend stays on this device. The daily puzzle adds one to two anonymous counts (solved, and solved first try). Games are deleted after 7 days. Nothing is used for ads or tracking. To keep out fake apps and bots, Google's reCAPTCHA checks in the background that requests come from the real Chess Quest app; it never shows a puzzle.

      Add friend

      Give them a nickname so you know who's who. It stays on this device: nobody else sees it.

      Privacy

      Chess Quest doesn't ask for your name, email or age, has no accounts and no chat, and shows no ads. Nothing is used to track you.

      On this device

      Your lesson stars, puzzle progress, daily streak, settings, saved games, friends' nicknames and two-player names are kept on this phone or tablet only. They're never sent anywhere. The app asks the browser to keep them even when the device is low on space. Your record against each friend and your badges are kept here too. If you allow notifications, "Your move" notifications are made by this device itself: no notification server or tracking ID is involved. Uninstalling the app or clearing the browser's site data deletes them.

      Online play (only if you use it)

      • A game stores: the moves, each player's avatar (one of 12 animals), the latest emoji reaction (one of six from a list, never typed text), the clock times, and a random player number the app makes for this device. It isn't linked to any name, email or account.
      • A seat key, a random code that lets this device rejoin its own game, is stored where nobody can read it.
      • Games are deleted after 7 days. Player numbers are deleted after 30 days. Friend links (a private code only the two devices know) are deleted after 60 days without use.
      • Daily puzzle: solving it adds one to two anonymous counts for that day (solved, and solved on the first try). Nothing else is stored.

      Services used

      • Google Firebase stores online games and the daily counts on Google's servers.
      • Google reCAPTCHA checks in the background that online requests come from the real app, not a bot. This site is protected by reCAPTCHA and the Google Privacy Policy and Terms of Service apply.
      • GitHub Pages hosts the app. Like any website host, it may keep short-term technical logs of visits.

      For parents

      Children can use every part of Chess Quest without giving any personal information. Online games are only with people you've shared a game code or friend link with: there's no matching with strangers and no way to send messages.

      Questions, or want online data removed sooner? Contact us on GitHub.
