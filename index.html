    What's stored?

    Only what's needed to play: the moves, each player's avatar, and a random player number made by the app (not linked to any account, email or name). Games are deleted after 7 days. Nothing is used for ads or tracking. To keep out fake apps and bots, Google's reCAPTCHA checks in the background that requests come from the real Chess Quest app; it never shows a puzzle.
